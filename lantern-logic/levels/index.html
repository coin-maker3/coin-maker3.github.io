      About the levels

      The 600 levels come in 20 packs of 30. Boards grow from gentle 5×5 grids to tricky 11×11 ones; every fifth level is a breather and every tenth a harder bonus. Solving a level unlocks the next one.

      Solve without hints or mistakes for three stars; one hint and up to three mistakes still earn two. You get three free hints a day, shared with the daily puzzle, and every board has exactly one solution that you can reach without guessing. New to the rules? Take the short tutorial or read how to play.

      Your stars, best times and unfinished board stay in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
