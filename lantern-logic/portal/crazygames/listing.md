# CrazyGames submission sheet

**Game name:** Lantern Logic: Queens Puzzle

**Short description (one line):**
Light one lantern in every row, column and colour, and never let two touch.

**Description:**
Lantern Logic is a calm logic puzzle in the Queens style. Each board is a
square grid split into coloured regions. Place lanterns so that every row,
every column and every colour holds exactly one lantern, and no two lanterns
touch, not even diagonally.

- 600 levels in 20 packs, from gentle 5×5 boards to tricky 11×11 ones
- Every puzzle has exactly one solution and never needs a guess
- Earn up to three stars per level: solve with no hints and no mistakes
- Hints explain the next logical step (three free hints a day)
- Auto-cross, undo, colour-blind patterns and a short tutorial
- Breather levels every fifth level, a harder bonus every tenth

**Controls:**
- Click or tap a cell: empty → cross → lantern → empty
- Drag across cells: cross out several cells at once
- Keyboard: arrow keys to move, Space or Enter to play a cell, Ctrl+Z to undo
- Hint, Undo and Restart buttons under the board
