# FY1 Guide — 2WW Colorectal Concordance Audit

Welcome. Thank you for helping with this audit. Should take about 5
minutes per case once you're warmed up. Aim for 25 cases per session.

## What you'll do

For each consecutive 2WW colorectal clinic letter:

1. Open the tool: **[audit URL]/#/audit/new**
2. Enter your initials and the clinic month
3. Read the clinic letter and fill in the symptom / blood / examination
   findings (no patient identifiers — see "What NOT to enter" below)
4. Once your data is in, the tool will show the **algorithm's
   recommended investigation** in the right-hand card
5. Then enter what was **actually decided** in the clinic letter outcome
6. The tool flags concordance (match) or mismatch
7. If it's a mismatch, write 1 sentence on why you think they differed
8. Click **Save audit case**
9. Move to the next letter

## What NOT to enter

- ❌ Patient name
- ❌ NHS number
- ❌ Hospital number
- ❌ Date of birth
- ❌ Specific clinic date (we use month-year only)
- ❌ Free-text identifiers (postcodes, addresses, phone)

The tool has no field for these. If you find yourself wanting to type
one of these somewhere, stop — the audit doesn't need it.

## What to do if…

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Patient didn't attend (DNA) — no outcome | **Exclude.** Don't save the case. |
| Patient already had cancer diagnosis — went straight to MDT | **Exclude.** No investigation pathway choice was applied. |
| Blood result missing in the letter | Leave the field empty. Tool handles missing values. |
| FIT not mentioned | Leave it empty. Tool will note "no FIT". |
| Multiple investigations chosen (e.g. col + CT) | Pick the *primary* investigation as the actual decision. Note the additional one in the outcome notes. |
| Outcome is "discuss at MDT" | Choose **Discuss at colorectal MDT** as the actual decision. |
| Outcome is "discharge to GP" | Choose **Discharge to GP with advice**. |
| You're not sure what the actual decision was | Skip the case and flag it for the audit lead. |
| You enter a case by mistake | Use the **Delete** button in the audit list. |

## Tips

- **One letter at a time.** Don't multitask.
- **Type fast.** The tool measures time per case — that's deliberate.
  Don't agonise over interpretation; if a finding is documented, enter
  it; if not, leave it blank.
- **Don't try to second-guess the algorithm.** Just enter what's in the
  letter. The tool will reveal its recommendation after you've finished
  data entry.
- **Trust your reading.** If the letter says "tenesmus", click yes. If
  it says "no tenesmus", click no. If it's not mentioned, don't infer —
  leave it.
- **Mismatch ≠ error.** Many mismatches are legitimate clinician
  overrides (patient preference, comorbidity, MDT decision). Those are
  what we want to learn from.

## Audit ID format

Your saved cases will be auto-numbered `AUDIT-2026-0001`,
`AUDIT-2026-0002`, etc. Note these in your own list if you want to come
back to a specific case.

## Time per case

Most cases take 3–5 minutes once you're warmed up. The first 5 will
take longer — that's expected.

## Daily checklist

- [ ] Sign in to Trust clinical portal
- [ ] Open audit tool: **[audit URL]/#/audit/new**
- [ ] Aim for 25 cases per session
- [ ] At the end of session: open the audit list page, sanity-check
      your entries
- [ ] Stop at the end of the agreed window

## Stuck?

WhatsApp the audit lead. Don't enter cases when you're unsure — flag
them.

## Privacy reminder

You are accessing real clinic letters as part of your normal clinical
duty. The tool you're typing into has been designed so that no
identifiable information enters it — only structured clinical findings.
The audit dataset that comes out the other end is fully anonymised and
will be shared with the Trust audit office.
