Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy covers Cart to Rocket on this website: the page you are reading, the game at the top of the home page, and the full-screen game at /cart-to-rocket/play/.

What we collect

Nothing. The game and these pages do not collect, send, sell or share any personal data or usage data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party scripts, fonts or embeds. The game makes no network requests after it has loaded.

Cookies

None. Neither the game nor these pages set any cookies.

Local storage

To remember your progress, the game saves one small record (about 1 KB) under the name ctr_save in your browser's local storage. It holds:

  • your coins, part levels and best distance;
  • which forts you have cleared and simple totals such as blocks smashed, runs played and time played;
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, reduce effects), which tips you have already seen, and when the record was created and last saved.

If a saved record is ever damaged, the game keeps a copy of it under ctr_save_corrupt and starts a fresh one.

This record stays on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time with Pause → Settings → Reset progress in the game, or by clearing this site's data in your browser. In a private window it is deleted when the window closes.

Vibration

On phones that support it, the game can vibrate briefly when you smash blocks or buy a part. This uses your browser's vibration feature only and can be turned off in Settings.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical data such as IP addresses in its server logs for security and operation; see GitHub's own privacy statement. We have no access to those logs and add nothing to them.

Advertising

This website shows no ads. Cart to Rocket may also be offered on third-party web game portals. A copy played on a portal runs inside that portal's page, which may show ads and may keep your progress in your portal account; that portal's own privacy policy applies there. The website you are reading has no ads and no portal code.

Children

Cart to Rocket is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date. For questions, open an issue on the website's public repository, coin-maker3/coin-maker3.github.io.
