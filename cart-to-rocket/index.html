Cart to Rocket: a free physics crash-and-launch game

Push a squeaky cart off the top of Suburb Hill, fly off the ramp and crash into forts built from real stacked blocks. Spend what you earn on parts, watch them bolt on, and go further every run, from a shopping cart to a go-kart to a pickup with twin rockets.

  • Free, no download
  • Phone, tablet and desktop
  • Runs of 15 to 50 seconds
  • No ads, no sign-up

What it is

A crash-and-upgrade arcade game with real rigid-body physics. Every crate can splinter, glass shatters, jelly wobbles and throws you back into the air, and a good hit brings a whole tower down.

Each run pays coins for distance, smashed blocks, combos, clean landings and backflips. In the garage you spend them on four parts: Wheels, Ram, Engine and Booster. Each has 16 levels and four looks, so a plank bumper becomes a snow plough, a steel wedge and finally a spinning drill nose.

Twelve forts stand between you and the Town Wall. After that, the Bonus Road keeps going.

How your progress is saved

Your coins, parts, best distance and settings are saved automatically in this browser's local storage on this device. There is no account and nothing is sent to a server.

  • Progress stays on this device and in this browser only.
  • Clearing this site's data, or a private window closing, erases it.
  • To start over, open Pause → Settings → Reset progress in the game.
